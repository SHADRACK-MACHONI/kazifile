Privacy & Data Handling

Last updated: September 2026

KaziFile exists to help you store and share sensitive personal documents — including identification, academic records, and certificates. Because of this, we take data protection seriously and want to be transparent about exactly how your information is handled.

What we collect

  • Your username, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
  • Documents you choose to upload, encrypted individually before storage
  • Metadata about your documents: title, category, and upload date
  • Access logs: when a document is shared or viewed, including the IP address of the request

How your documents are protected

  • Every document is encrypted with its own unique encryption key before being saved to storage
  • Share links expire automatically after 2 days
  • You can enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for additional login security
  • Failed login attempts are rate-limited to protect against unauthorized access

How we use your data

Your documents and personal information are used solely to provide the KaziFile service to you — storing your files, generating share links, and maintaining your access history. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or documents with third parties for marketing purposes.

Your rights

You may delete any document from your account at any time, which permanently removes both the file and its encryption key. If you wish to delete your entire account and all associated data, please contact us using the details below.

Compliance with Kenyan law

KaziFile is built with an awareness of the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya, which governs the collection, processing, and storage of personal data within the country. As an evolving student project, KaziFile is not yet formally registered with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC), but is designed in line with the Act's core principles — including data minimization, purpose limitation, and security safeguards for personal data.

Data retention

Documents remain stored until you delete them yourself. Access logs are retained to give you full visibility into your document's history and are not currently subject to automatic deletion.

Contact

For questions about this policy or your data, or to request account deletion, please reach out through the contact details associated with this project.
